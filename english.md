Leper returned after mastering his skills, becoming a sword immortal!
Leper sword immortal can fly on his sword, moving with incredible agility.
Leper sword immortal attacks with his flying sword, striking any target from any location, with significantly increased accuracy.

[img]https://images.steamusercontent.com/ugc/18407363087148070422/203A6799A4C15D89063E3EDE58EA91050EDE8478/[/img]
I've always liked Leper, but he's just too difficult to use, so I sent him off to cultivate immortality. Now he's back!
This is a mod that completely changes how you play Leper, including:
[list]
[*] Remake 7 combat skills
[*] Add five new camping skills
[*] 3 exclusive virtues and 3 exclusive afflictions
[*] 7 exclusive positive quirks and 3 exclusive negative quirks
[/list]
[u][b]This mod can be played without DLC, but the experience is better with DLC enabled.[/b][/u]
