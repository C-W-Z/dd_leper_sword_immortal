[h2]Introduction[/h2]
This mod modifies the Old Road party into a Leper Sword Immortal (no quirks).
Reynauld (Crusader) and Dismas (Highwayman) are already waiting for you in Hamlet. This mod only works when starting a new game.

[h2]Prerequisite Mods[/h2]
Requires the Leper Sword Immortal Mod to be enabled simultaneously.

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
Incompatible with other mods that modify the Old Road party.
Incompatible with other Leper enhancement mods.

[h2]Suggested Mod Order[/h2]
No special requirements; it can be placed above or below the Leper Sword Immortal Mod.
